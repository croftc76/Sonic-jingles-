🎮 ROBLOX TALENT HUB
A Comprehensive Platform Review
BAD QUALITIES
Bad Quality #1
⚠️
Forced Migration Disaster
On October 1, 2021, Roblox permanently locked the DevForum collaboration section and forced developers to migrate to Talent Hub before it was ready. Many developers begged Roblox to delay the migration, but were ignored. The platform had substantial limitations and users reported ZERO job offers after migration, compared to successfully finding work through the forum.
Bad Quality #2
💸
Exploitative Pay Rates
Job postings often offer less than minimum wage. Example: "Design an entire high-poly map for R$300" - that's just $3 USD! People confuse Robux with real money: 4,000 Robux might sound generous, but it's only $4. It's borderline illegal exploitation of developers.
Bad Quality #3
🤥
Lying About Payment
Job posts frequently lie about compensation. They'll advertise an exact price to attract applicants, then change the terms to "payment will be based on your work" once you apply. This bait-and-switch tactic wastes developers' time and creates distrust.
Bad Quality #4
✅
Misleading "Verified" Badge
Roblox's Help page states that verified users are "users that Roblox knows and trusts" - but verification only checks age and identity, NOT skills or reliability! Anyone can get verified immediately after creating their account with zero experience. This gives users a false sense of security.
Bad Quality #5
🚨
Scammers Exploit Verification
The verification system does NOTHING to prevent scamming. Scammers deliberately seek verification because the badge makes them appear more trustworthy to potential victims. The algorithm also keeps shoving verified posts down your throat even when you disable "ID Verified Only" filter.
Bad Quality #6
💬
No Comment System
Unlike DevForum's collaboration section, there's NO way to comment on posts. You can't ask questions publicly, can't verify if someone is trustworthy, and can't warn others about bad experiences. The lack of community feedback removes natural accountability and makes the platform feel cold and isolated.
Bad Quality #7
🔍
Completely Broken Search
The search system is fundamentally broken. Filter by tags for specific skills? Half the time results don't match. The search prioritizes popular posts regardless of relevance to your criteria. Meanwhile, DevForum's search works perfectly fine. Why can't Talent Hub get this basic feature right?
Bad Quality #8
🐛
Constant 404/504 Errors
Try loading a job post, application, or creator profile? Good luck! You'll frequently get 404 or 504 errors. Sometimes refreshing helps. Sometimes it doesn't. It's 2025 and basic page loading is still broken on a professional networking platform.
Bad Quality #9
⛔
"You Are Not Qualified" Block
Worst case scenario: you try to open Talent Hub and get hit with "You are not qualified" error, blocking ALL access to the site. Not to a specific job - to the ENTIRE platform. This error can appear randomly and lock you out of your professional networking tool.
Bad Quality #10
🔒
Chats Won't Open
Users report that when they try to open chats in the inbox, they can't access them on the first try. You need to refresh the page multiple times until the chat finally opens. Sometimes it takes 5, 10, or more refreshes. Imagine trying to conduct professional business like this!
Bad Quality #11
👻
Messages Disappear Forever
You type a message and hit send. It appears briefly... then VANISHES. Refresh the page? Gone forever. This makes it impossible for job creators to communicate with applicants. Your carefully written professional messages just disappear into the void.
Bad Quality #12
📱
Mobile Browsers Are Useless
On mobile browsers, you can navigate to applications but you CANNOT view any messages or conversation history associated with them. The messaging feature is completely non-functional on mobile. In 2025, this is inexcusable for a professional platform.
Bad Quality #13
⚡
Random "Messaging Unavailable"
The system randomly shows "Messaging unavailable" even for people who were chatting with you before. New messages come in but the error doesn't go away. You're locked out of conversations with no explanation or way to fix it.
Bad Quality #14
📄
Blank Conversation Screens
Sometimes conversations show up in your inbox - you can see you have a conversation - but when you click it, the screen is completely blank. You know messages exist. You just can't see them. It's like trying to read invisible ink.
Bad Quality #15
🚫
Completely Locked Out of Messaging
Worst case scenario: some users can't send messages to ANYONE they applied to. Not just one person. Everyone. You apply to jobs but can never communicate with potential employers. The entire platform becomes useless.
Bad Quality #16
🔇
Chat Filter Blocks Job Info
The same chat filter from Roblox games censors PROFESSIONAL information. Basic job-related terms get filtered out, making it impossible to communicate with clients or employers. Words that aren't offensive get censored in a business setting. DevForum doesn't have this issue.
Bad Quality #17
💔
Can't Exchange Contact Info
You literally CANNOT exchange Discord usernames, email addresses, or other contact info. This is devastating because Discord is the standard communication platform for Roblox developers. The filter forces you to use third-party platforms, then punishes you for trying to mention them!
Bad Quality #18
📧
Rejection Emails Without Offers
You get an email: "Sorry, you weren't selected for this job." Wait, what job? You never received an email about the original job offer! You discover missed opportunities only AFTER being rejected. You never had a chance to respond.
Bad Quality #19
📭
Notification Emails Don't Send
Even with properly configured notification settings, emails just DON'T send. Candidates think recruiters haven't responded when they actually have. One manager thought their post had no responses for DAYS, only to discover 40+ applications hidden in the broken inbox.
Bad Quality #20
🎨
Zero Portfolio Customization
The base layout is identical across ALL creator pages. No customization options. Every portfolio looks exactly the same. This is so limiting that developers created external tools like "Bloxfolio" just to have proper portfolio customization!
Bad Quality #21
🔲
Images Locked to Squares
You CANNOT control image sizing - everything is locked into a square shape. This destroys wide thumbnails, UI designs, and any rectangular work. Developers with 16:9 showcase images are forced to crop them into awkward squares that ruin the composition.
Bad Quality #22
✂️
300 Characters vs 5,000
Each experience description has a 300-character limit. DevForum allowed 5,000 characters! You can't properly describe your work, explain your role, or detail technical challenges. Professional portfolios require context - 300 characters is barely a tweet.
Bad Quality #23
🔟
Only 10 Experiences Allowed
You can only showcase 10 experiences total on your portfolio. Many experienced developers have dozens or hundreds of projects. Being forced to pick only 10 severely limits your ability to demonstrate the breadth of your skills and experience.
Bad Quality #24
🎬
No Videos, Audio, or Documents
You can't upload videos, audio files, or documents. This is DEVASTATING for animators who need to show movement, UI designers demonstrating interactions, scripters showing game mechanics, and music producers showcasing their work. Static images don't cut it.
Bad Quality #25
⚠️
Image Moderation False Warnings
Image moderation is ABSOLUTELY THE WORST. Users get false warnings every 2 weeks on average. Professional portfolio images - UI mockups, game screenshots, 3D models - flagged as inappropriate for no reason. Your account gets strikes for legitimate work.
Bad Quality #26
⏳
Images Stuck for 180+ Days
Images get stuck in "Pending Moderation" for MONTHS. Some posts have been stuck for over 180 days! Every attempt to post images gets stuck indefinitely, making it impossible to create or update your portfolio. Your professional presence is frozen.
Bad Quality #27
📨
No Bulk Messaging
Unlike every other modern job platform, there's NO way to send messages to multiple applicants at once. You must message each person individually. With 20+ candidates? That's 20+ copy-paste operations. It's tedious, time-consuming, and unprofessional.
Bad Quality #28
📱
Hostile Mobile UI
The mobile UI is poorly designed and user-hostile. The message interface has a terrible ratio of used to unused space - cramped where it matters, wasteful everywhere else. Navigating through responses is difficult and uncomfortable.
Bad Quality #29
🏢
Can't Add Group Games
Developers can't add games they've worked on from a group to their profile unless their account owns the group. Error: "Failure to Add Experience. Ensure that the Experience ID is valid." The only workaround? Hope the owner cooperates and adds games to the group's creator page first.
Bad Quality #30
🚫
No Block or Mute Feature
There's NO way to block or mute someone in your inbox. The only workarounds? Report them (might not work) or disable ALL notifications. Blocking users on main Roblox doesn't affect Talent Hub. This basic safety feature has been standard on social platforms since 2005!
Bad Quality #31
🔗
Can't Contact Inactive Creators
You CANNOT contact or reach out to creators unless they currently have an active job posting live on the platform. This is frustrating given the moderation queue delays and the fact that the best talent often isn't actively posting. Professional networking is impossible.
✨
Good Qualities
Despite all these issues, the underlying idea of a dedicated platform for Roblox developers is genuinely valuable. Some people HAVE successfully found work through the platform. The compensation filter feature (when it works) is useful. When everything works as intended, it provides a centralized place for Roblox-specific opportunities that might not exist elsewhere.

🎮 ROBLOX TALENT HUB
A Professional Platform Review
BAD QUALITIES
Bad Quality #1
⚠️
Forced Migration Disaster
Roblox permanently locked the DevForum collaboration section on October 1, 2021, forcing developers onto Talent Hub before it was ready. Many developers reported zero job offers after the migration, compared to successfully finding work through the old forum system. The rushed transition disrupted established workflows and left many unable to network effectively.
Bad Quality #2
💰
No Quality Control on Job Posts
Job postings have no quality control. Recruiters often offer work for less than minimum wage (e.g., designing an entire high-poly map for R$300, which is just $3). People confuse Robux amounts with real money - 4,000 Robux sounds like a lot but it's only $4. Posts often lie about compensation, promising exact prices then saying "payment based on your work."
Bad Quality #3
✅
Misleading Verification System
Roblox claims verified users are "users that Roblox knows and trusts," but verification only checks age and identity, not actual skills or reliability. Anyone can get verified immediately after creating their account with zero experience. Scammers deliberately seek verification to appear trustworthy. The algorithm also favors verified posts even when you turn off "ID Verified Only."
Bad Quality #4
💬
No Comment System
There's no way to comment on any post, unlike the DevForum's collaboration section. This makes it impossible to verify if an applicant is trustworthy before contacting them. Without comments, there's no way for the community to warn each other about bad experiences or share advice. The lack of public discussion prevents good behavior from being recognized and bad behavior from being called out.
Bad Quality #5
🔍
Broken Search System
The search system is completely broken. When you filter by tags for specific skills, half the time the results don't match what you're looking for. The search shows popular posts regardless of whether they fit your criteria. Oddly enough, DevForum doesn't have this problem at all.
Bad Quality #6
🐛
Plagued with Bugs
The platform suffers from constant bugs: 404/504 errors when loading posts, applications, or profiles. Sometimes you get "You are not qualified" errors blocking all access. Users can't open chats without refreshing multiple times. Job creators can't see most applicants, and applicants can't see their own applications.
Bad Quality #7
📧
Messaging System Disaster
Messages vanish when sent and disappear forever after refreshing. Mobile browsers can't view message history. "Messaging unavailable" appears randomly. Conversations show up blank. Worst case: users can't message ANYONE they applied to. The chat filter censors job-related information and even contact details like Discord usernames, making professional communication impossible.
Bad Quality #8
🔔
Broken Notification System
You get emails about job rejections but never about the original job offers, so you discover missed opportunities only after getting declined. Even with proper notification settings, emails don't send. One manager thought their post had no responses for days, only to discover 40+ hidden applications in the broken inbox.
Bad Quality #9
🎨
Severely Limited Portfolios
The base layout is identical across all creator pages with no customization (developers created "Bloxfolio" just to have proper portfolios). Images are locked to square shapes, destroying wide thumbnails and UI designs. Only 10 experiences allowed with 300-character descriptions (DevForum allowed 5,000). No videos, audio, or documents - terrible for animators, UI designers, and music producers.
Bad Quality #10
🖼️
Image Moderation Hell
Image moderation is absolutely the worst. Users get false warnings every 2 weeks. Images get stuck in "Pending Moderation" for months - some posts have been stuck for over 180 days, making it impossible to post anything as every attempt gets stuck indefinitely.
Bad Quality #11
⚙️
More Problems...
No bulk messaging (message each applicant individually). Mobile UI is cramped and hostile. Can't add group games to your profile unless you own the group. No way to block or mute users in your inbox. Can't contact creators unless they have an active job posting. The platform is fundamentally broken for professional networking.
Good Qualities
The underlying idea of a dedicated platform for Roblox developers is valuable. Some people have successfully found work. The compensation filter feature is useful (when it works). When the platform works as intended, it provides a centralized place for Roblox-specific opportunities.

About This Jingle

This hypothetical startup jingle represents what could have been an alternate boot sequence for the NES. Due to the extreme storage limitations of the NES (only 2KB of RAM!), this more elaborate jingle was supposedly cut in favor of silence or simpler audio cues.

The jingle uses authentic NES sound chip characteristics:

  • Square wave channels (pulse waves)
  • Triangle wave for bass
  • Simple melodic patterns
  • 8-bit charm

About These Jingles

NES - Nintendo Entertainment System

This hypothetical startup jingle represents what could have been an alternate boot sequence for the NES. Due to the extreme storage limitations of the NES (only 2KB of RAM!), this more elaborate jingle was supposedly cut in favor of silence or simpler audio cues.

Sound Chip: 2A03 (Square waves, Triangle, Noise)

Master System - SEGA

An epic hypothetical startup sequence for the Sega Master System featuring a catchy melody, a surprising key change, and triumphant SEGA-inspired chords. The Master System also had no startup sound, but this imagines what could have been!

Sound Chip: SN76489 (3x Square waves + Noise)

Special Features: Key change from C Major to E Major, dramatic chord progression
